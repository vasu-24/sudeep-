What RMS Is, In Plain Words

Read this one section and you will know exactly what we do, who we do it for, what you receive, and how to begin.

RMS is a steel detailing and architectural drafting studio. A building starts as an architect's idea and an engineer's calculation. Neither of those can be taken to a workshop and built. Our job is the step in between: we convert design intent into a precise 3D model and into drawings so detailed that every plate, bolt, weld and cut length is already decided before steel is touched.

A

What we actually do

We build a constructible 3D model of your structure in Tekla or Revit, resolve every clash inside the computer, and produce the full drawing set that a fabrication shop and an erection crew work from.

B

Who we work for

Architects, structural consultants, steel fabricators, general contractors, and factory owners. If you are building anything with a steel frame, you are who this page is for.

C

What you receive

General Arrangement drawings, assembly and single-part shop drawings, connection details, erection plans, CNC files (NC1 / DXF), bill of materials, and the native 3D model file.

D

How to begin

Send your design drawings and specifications using the form in Section 12. We reply within 24 working hours with scope, price, and a delivery schedule. No cost to ask.

06 Process Section 06 of 12

How We Work

Five Steps From Your File To The Site

Every project we take moves through the same five stages. You always know which stage your job is sitting in.

Step 01

Understand

We read your schematics, project boundaries and standards, then confirm the scope in writing.

Step 02

Plan

Model protocols, drawing standards, naming rules and delivery milestones are locked before work starts.

Step 03

Model

A high-detail 3D model is built and every clash is resolved inside the software, not on site.

Step 04

Detail

Fabrication sheets, GA sets and connection details are generated from the approved model.

Step 05

Deliver

A three-pass quality check runs, then the full package and native model files are handed over.
