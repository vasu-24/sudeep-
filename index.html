What RMS Is, In Plain Words

Read this one section and you will know what we do, which of our two divisions you need, what you receive, and how to begin.

A building passes through two very different kinds of drawing. First an architect decides the shape, the spaces and the look. Then a detailer decides every plate, bolt, weld and cut length so a workshop can actually fabricate the steel. Most firms do one or the other. RMS runs both, as two dedicated divisions with their own teams, software and standards.

A

What we actually do

We design buildings (Division 01) and we detail the steel that holds them up (Division 02). Each division is explained in full in Sections 04 and 05.

B

Who we work for

Developers and owners who need a design. Architects and structural consultants who need detailing support. Steel fabricators and contractors who need shop-ready drawings.

C

What you receive

From Division 01: concept sets, working drawings, approval drawings and 3D visuals. From Division 02: 3D models, GA drawings, shop drawings, CNC files and a bill of materials.

D

How to begin

Go to Section 12, pick which division you need, and send your files. We reply within 24 working hours with scope, price and a delivery schedule. Asking costs nothing.

06 About Us Section 06 of 12

The Studio

One Office Behind Both Divisions

A drawing is only good if the workshop can build from it without a single phone call. That is the standard both divisions are held to.

RMS Bureau Floor

Why we run two divisions

Architecture and steel detailing use different software, different codes and a different way of thinking. Trying to do both with one team produces weak versions of each. So we separated them: an architecture team that designs, and a detailing team that fabricates on paper. They sit in the same office and share the same model server.

Fifteen years, four hundred and fifty projects, and thirty engineers across Europe, North America and Asia. Every job in either division runs under the same checklist, the same naming rules and the same three-pass review before anything leaves our office.

What both divisions share

  • • Three-pass quality review
  • • Shared 3D model server
  • • Tracked revision history
  • • 24-hour reply promise
  • • Native file handover

08 Process Section 08 of 12

How We Work

Two Divisions, Two Timelines

Each division runs its own five stages. If you hire both, stage 05 of Division 01 feeds straight into stage 01 of Division 02.

Division 01

Architecture

Step 01

Brief

Site data, your requirements, budget and local building rules are collected.

Step 02

Concept

Massing and layout options are drawn and reviewed with you before detail begins.

Step 03

Design

Plans, elevations and sections are developed with structure and services coordinated.

Step 04

Approval

Permit drawings are formatted and submitted to the relevant authority.

Step 05

Issue

Working drawings and 3D visuals are released for construction.

Output of Division 01 becomes the input of Division 02
Division 02

Steel Detailing

Step 01

Understand

We read your schematics, project boundaries and standards, then confirm scope in writing.

Step 02

Plan

Model protocols, naming rules and delivery milestones are locked before work starts.

Step 03

Model

A high-detail 3D model is built and every clash is resolved inside the software.

Step 04

Detail

Shop sheets, GA sets and connection details are generated from the approved model.

Step 05

Deliver

Three-pass quality check runs, then CNC files and native models are handed over.
