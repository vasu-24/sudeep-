What We Do, In Plain Words

Read this one section and you will know exactly what we do, who we do it for, what you receive, and how to begin.

RMS Steel Detailing turns design into fabrication. A building starts as an architect's drawing and an engineer's calculation. Neither of those can be taken to a workshop and built. Our job is the step in between: we turn design intent into a precise 3D steel model and into drawings so detailed that every plate, bolt, weld and cut length is already decided before steel is touched.

A

What we actually do

We build a constructible 3D model of your steel structure in Tekla or Revit, resolve every clash inside the software, and produce the full drawing set that a fabrication shop and an erection crew work from.

B

Who we work for

Steel fabricators, structural consultants, general contractors, erection crews and factory owners. If you are building anything with a steel frame, this page is for you.

C

What you receive

General Arrangement drawings, assembly and single-part shop drawings, connection details, erection plans, CNC files (NC1 / DXF), bill of materials, and the native 3D model file.

D

How to begin

Send your design drawings and specifications using the form in Section 12. We reply within 24 working hours with scope, price and a delivery schedule. Asking costs nothing.

How it works

Three simple steps

  1. 01 You send design drawings

    Architectural and structural sets, your specifications, and the dates you are working to.

  2. 02 We model and detail the steel

    A constructible 3D model in Tekla or Revit, with every clash resolved before drawings are issued.

  3. 03 You receive fabrication files

    Shop drawings, GA sets, connection details, CNC files and a bill of materials.

06 Process Section 06 of 12

How We Work

Five Steps From Your File To The Site

Every project moves through the same five stages. You always know which stage your job is sitting in.

Step 01

Understand

We read your schematics, project boundaries and standards, then confirm the scope in writing.

Step 02

Plan

Model protocols, drawing standards, naming rules and delivery milestones are locked before work starts.

Step 03

Model

A high-detail 3D steel model is built and every clash is resolved inside the software, not on site.

Step 04

Detail

Shop sheets, GA sets and connection details are generated from the approved model.

Step 05

Deliver

A three-pass quality check runs, then CNC files, drawings and native models are handed over.
